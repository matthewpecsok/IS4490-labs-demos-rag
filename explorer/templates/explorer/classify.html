Semantic search
doesn't scale to
50,000 resumes.

One semantic search can't reliably rank every relevant document out of tens of thousands. Instead, classify each resume once: retrieve the most relevant chunks within that single resume, ask Gemini a yes/no qualification question grounded in that evidence, and store the boolean. Answering "who qualifies?" afterward is a plain database query, not another semantic search.
